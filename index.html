Why Tour Ceylon

Smarter, safer travel across the island

Sri Lanka offers heritage, wildlife, beaches and wellness—but visitors need timely context on crowd pressure, emergencies and personalised plans. Our four research components address that gap with honest evaluation and practical APIs.

How it fits together

From data to decisions on the ground

Gather context

Site metadata, seasonal signals and platform inputs are cleaned and merged into shared datasets.

Model & evaluate

Each component trains and reports results with holdout tests suited to its problem—not only random splits.

Expose APIs

Flask services and a React front end let travellers and supervisors explore outputs in one place.

Inform the journey

Risk scores, SOS flows, itineraries and wellness tips support safer, richer visits across Sri Lanka.
